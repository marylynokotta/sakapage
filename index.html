About Bukayo Saka

Player Profile

Born: September 4, 2001 — Ealing, London

Height: 178cm | Weight: 72kg

Preferred Foot: Left

Jersey: #7 Arsenal | #7 or #11 England

Contract: Arsenal until June 2030

Club Career

Joined Arsenal's Hale End Academy at age 8 in 2010. Made his senior debut at 17 in November 2018. Named Arsenal Player of the Year in 2020/21 and 2021/22. Helped Arsenal end a 22-year league drought by winning the 2025–26 Premier League title.

Milestone

Became the second-youngest player in Premier League history to reach 50+ goals and 50+ assists, trailing only Wayne Rooney. Scored the decisive goal vs Atlético Madrid to send Arsenal to their first Champions League final in 20 years.

International Career

Senior England debut in October 2020. Bounced back from a missed Euro 2020 final penalty to win England Men's Player of the Year in 2022 and 2023. Scored 3 goals at the 2022 World Cup. Arsenal's all-time leading scorer for England.

Personal Life

Raised in Greenford, London by Nigerian parents of Yoruba descent and proud of his cultural roots. Was an exceptional student at Greenford High School. In late 2025, announced his engagement to long-time girlfriend Tolami Benson.
